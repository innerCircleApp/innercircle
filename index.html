Questions

Before you join.

Who can join InnerCircle+?

Adults aged 20 and over who complete a live selfie verification. Sign in is with your Apple ID.

How does matching work?

Each day you receive a small set of introductions. Send a love to someone who stands out. If they love you back, it’s a match and a private chat opens for both of you.

What happens to my verification selfie?

It is compared with your profile photos on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device technology, then discarded. We never store face data, only whether verification passed.

Is there a subscription?

No. InnerCircle+ is a one-time purchase on the App Store, with no ads and no recurring fees.

Can other members see where I am?

No. Your location is stored rounded to about a kilometer, and members only see an approximate distance. You can also hide your profile from new introductions at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, then Delete Account. Your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, and shared media are permanently deleted.
