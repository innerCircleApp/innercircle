Quick answers

Common questions.

How do matches work?

Tap the heart on someone in Today’s Introductions. They’re notified, and if they love you back, it’s a match and a private chat opens for both of you.

Where can I see who loved me?

Open Chats and tap the bell in the top corner. Tap anyone in the list to view their profile and love them back.

Why do I only see a few introductions a day?

InnerCircle+ is curated on purpose. Each day you get a small set of verified members chosen around your preferences and distance. When you’ve seen them all, new introductions arrive the next day.

How does verification work?

During setup you take a live selfie. Your iPhone compares it with your profile photos and then discards it. We never store face data, only whether verification passed.

How do I report or block someone?

In a chat, long press any message to report it, or tap the menu in the top corner to block the member or report harassment or fraud. Blocking takes effect immediately.

Can I control what others see?

Yes. In Settings, under Privacy, you can hide your profile from new introductions, turn read receipts off, and hide message text on your lock screen.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, then Delete Account. Your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, and shared media are permanently deleted, along with your sign-in account.

If you are ever in danger, contact your local emergency services first. Then let us know so we can act on the account involved.
